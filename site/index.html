Sarissa / Standalone intelligence

From evidence to a controlled response.

Sarissa brings reported security events, defensive planning, human decisions and evidence records into one coherent workflow.

Evidence first
Separate reported facts from verified outcomes.
Human authority
Review proposed changes before recording any decision.
Traceable outcomes
Preserve the event, plan and decision in an exportable record.
01 / Capabilities

Four stages. One controlled workflow.

Record a manually reported event, classify its impact and establish a local receipt.
Build a non-executing defensive review plan from the recorded event.
Review the plan and explicitly record approval or decline. No external actions are executed.
Export the event, plan, decision and provenance as JSON.
02 / Use cases

Clarity before action.

Choose a fictional scenario to explore a controlled review in the local workspace.

Service interruption
Record a reported interruption and organize an impact review.
Reported security concern
Preserve the reported concern and plan a human review.
03 / Resources

Preserve the record.

Practical guidance for the standalone Sarissa workflow.

Getting started

Enter Sarissa, select a role for orientation, acknowledge the local demo scope, and open the workspace.

Evidence and classification

Use fictional or non-sensitive information. Manually entered events are user-reported; a local receipt does not independently verify them.

Approval and scope

A recorded decision is local only. No external action, access change, or network operation is executed.

Access and privacy

This standalone build does not connect to a live identity provider or submit access requests. A downloaded draft can be reviewed offline.
